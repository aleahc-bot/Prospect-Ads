Common questions

Are there long-term contracts?

We went through 12 marketing companies in 8 years, so we don’t want to hold anyone who isn’t happy. We don’t do contracts. We use written agreements: 12 months, with the option to cancel after the first 3 months if you’re unhappy. And really, if you ever want to end the agreement, you can. We just believe in putting it in writing.

Can I start with one service and add more later?

Yes. Most clients start with one and add others once they see results.

How soon will I see results?

The results depend on which services you choose, your budget, and how fast you want to go to market. Whatever you pick, serious compounding results take 12 months or more, and months 24, 36 and 48 are when the snowball effect kicks in and the business becomes hard to stop. The agencies we hired promised a transformed business in 3, 6 or 9 months. That isn’t true, and we won’t tell you it is.
